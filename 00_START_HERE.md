## 📊 What Gets Analyzed

### 20+ Metrics Calculated

**Profitability (5)**
- Gross profit margin, operating margin, net margin, ROE, ROA

**Liquidity (3)**
- Current ratio, quick ratio, cash ratio

**Efficiency (3)**
- Asset turnover, receivables turnover, inventory turnover

**Leverage (4)**
- Debt-to-equity, debt-to-assets, equity ratio, interest coverage

**Cash Flow (3)**
- OCF margin, free cash flow, cash conversion metrics

### Generated Insights

**Strengths** (6 items)
- Profitability, liquidity, leverage, efficiency, growth, cash generation

**Concerns** (4 items)
- Potential weaknesses, areas needing improvement

**Risks** (5 items)
- Economic, industry, competitive, market, operational risks
- Plus mitigation strategies

**Recommendations**
- BUY/HOLD/SELL with 50-95% confidence
- Investment thesis (5 points)
- 8 action items with priorities
- 3 scoring metrics (financial health, growth, risk/reward)
